# Cardleaf Privacy Policy

[한국어](privacy-ko.html)

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Cardleaf saves the paper business cards you receive as a contact file and a card image. Everything happens on your phone. The developer does not collect or receive any of your information.

## Information collected

None. The app has no server, no accounts, and no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your data over the internet.

## Information the app handles on the phone

- **Camera**: used only while you photograph a card. The photo and the text recognition stay on the phone, in the app's temporary folder, and are deleted when you scan the next card or open the app again.
- **Photos**: "사진 가져오기" (Import photo) reads only the one photo you pick in the system picker; the app never reads the rest of your library. A card image is added to Photos only when you tap Save or turn on auto-save.
- **Contact file**: a vCard is created only when you tap "연락처 파일" (Contact file) and goes only to the app you choose. The app does not read or change your contacts.
- **Settings**: the app stores one setting on the phone, whether auto-save is on.

## When information reaches other services

The app sends nothing itself. Images saved to Photos may sync to iCloud Photos or another backup service according to your phone's settings, and images or contact files you share are handled by the app you share them with.

## Children

The app collects no one's personal information, children's included.
